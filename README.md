# AI Automation Job Market Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes the impact of Artificial Intelligence and automation on the global job market. The dashboard explores how AI adoption influences industries, job roles, salary trends, and workforce skill gaps across multiple countries.

## Problem Statement

The rapid growth of AI and automation is transforming the workforce across industries. Many job roles face increasing automation risk while new skill demands are emerging. Understanding how AI affects employment, salaries, and skill requirements is essential for businesses and professionals preparing for the future of work.

## Objective

The goal of this project is to analyze and visualize how AI and automation are impacting the global job market.

Key objectives include:

* Identify industries with the highest automation risk
* Analyze job roles vulnerable to AI replacement
* Compare AI adoption levels across countries
* Examine salary changes before and after AI adoption
* Identify workforce skill gaps

## Dashboard Features

* KPI cards for Automation Risk, AI Replacement Score, Salary Impact, and AI Adoption
* Industry-wise automation risk analysis
* Job role salary comparison before and after AI adoption
* Country-wise AI adoption level visualization
* Skill gap index analysis across job roles
* Interactive slicers for Country, Industry, and Automation Risk Category

## Key Insights

* Manufacturing and Energy show the highest automation risk (around 47%)
* AI adoption levels vary across countries, with India showing higher adoption
* Some job roles experience significant skill gaps due to technological change
* Automation influences salary trends differently across industries
